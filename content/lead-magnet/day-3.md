Chatbots like ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude are trained on huge amounts of text to predict what words come next. That makes them fluent, fast and useful for drafts, summaries and ideas.

It also means that sounding right and being right are two different things. These tools can state wrong facts in a confident voice. Researchers call this "hallucination", and it is a known, studied weakness, not a rare glitch.

A good rule: use AI to start, use your own judgement to finish.

Today's experiment: ask an AI tool a question you already know the answer to well. Check its answer line by line. Where was it right? Where was it confidently wrong?
