Neuroplasticity means the brain changes with experience, all through life. One well-known study scanned adults before and after three months of learning to juggle. Small areas involved in tracking motion grew measurably, then shrank back after they stopped practising.

Two honest notes. These changes are modest and specific to the skill practised. And "brain training" games mostly make you better at the games, not at everything else.

So the lesson is simple: you get better at what you actually repeat. Hours of quick scrolling train quick switching. Regular deep reading trains sustained focus. You are always training something.

Today's experiment: pick one small skill you want to keep sharp (mental arithmetic, a language, finding a route without maps) and practise it for 5 minutes.
