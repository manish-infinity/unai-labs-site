The habits with the strongest evidence for a healthy brain are not apps or supplements. They are sleep, regular physical activity and time with other people.

Sleep is when the brain consolidates what you learned during the day. In one trial, a year of brisk walking three times a week grew the hippocampus, a memory area, by about 2% in older adults. And the 2024 Lancet Commission estimated that about 45% of dementia cases worldwide are linked to 14 risk factors we can change, including physical inactivity and social isolation. That is an estimate across whole populations, not a promise for one person, but it points the same way.

Today's experiment: tonight, put your phone outside the bedroom and go to bed 20 minutes earlier. Tomorrow, take a 20-minute walk with someone you like.
