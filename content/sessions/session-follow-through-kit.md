# Session follow-through kit (roadmap R-18)

Built 15 Nov 2026 by the roadmap runner, for the free "Your Brain vs AI" awareness sessions (outreach v2, D-007). Purpose: turn one talk into a week of practice and into word of mouth. No selling: no prices, no product names, no buy links.

## Files
- `7-day-challenge-handout.pdf`: one A4 page. Hand out at the end, or the host shares it after the talk. Same three habits as slide 17 of the deck, each with the same named source, a 7-day tick grid, three optional extras and a day-7 reflection line.
- `feedback-slip.pdf`: A4 with two identical anonymous slips (cut on the dotted line). Three questions.
- Source: `tools/build_session_kit.py`. Edit text there and re-run; the build fails if an em or en dash appears.

## Feedback form (same 3 questions, for an online form or to read aloud)
Title: Your Brain vs AI: three quick questions
Intro: Anonymous. No name or contact details needed. Thank you for listening.
1. How useful was this talk for you? (scale 1 to 5; 1 = not useful, 5 = very useful)
2. Which one habit will you try this week? (short answer)
3. Is there another class, club or team that should hear this talk? (optional, name only)

Why these three: Q1 is the quality score we track per session; Q2 commits the listener to one habit (the point of the talk); Q3 is the word-of-mouth lever, asking for a group name only so no personal data is collected.

## How to run it (Manish, at the session)
1. On slide 17, say: "There is a one-page sheet with these three habits and a tick grid. Try it for 7 days."
2. Leave 2 minutes before Q&A ends for the slips. Ask the host to collect them, or show the online form if the host prefers.
3. After the session, log in OUTREACH STATE: attendees (approx), slips returned, average Q1, count of each Q2 habit, every Q3 group name. A Q3 name is a warm lead for a new session request, which still follows D-007 (one email, no follow-ups, from manish.singh@unai-labs.com, never Wipro companies or clients).

## Metric
Per session: average usefulness (target 4.0+), share of slips naming a habit (target 70%+), Q3 group names (target 1+). Logged as part of X-002.
