# Talk deck: "Your Brain vs AI" (free awareness session, roadmap R-15)

Built 13 Nov 2026 by the roadmap runner. File: content/sessions/your-brain-vs-ai-talk.pptx (18 slides, 16:9, speaker notes on every slide) and a PDF copy. Source of the slides: tools/build_talk_deck.py (edit there, re-run, never hand-edit the pptx). Manish delivers it; nothing here is sent to anyone.

Rules met: no sales slide, no prices, no product names, no em-dashes (the build script fails if one appears), every evidence slide carries a named source line, early or small studies are labelled as such.

## Variants (same deck, swap emphasis; no new claims needed)
- **Students, "Study smarter with your brain"** (exam season): title slide subtitle "Study smarter with your brain"; spend extra time on slides 8, 10, 14, 15 (retrieval, spacing, Study 1 is about students); slide 16 note: all-nighters before exams.
- **Companies, "Use AI without losing your edge"**: subtitle "Use AI without losing your edge"; spend extra time on slides 5, 12, 13, 14 (attention residue, Study 3 is about knowledge workers, "stay the editor"); habit 1 example: write your own 3 points before asking AI for a draft.
- **Wellbeing, "Focus, sleep and stress: what the brain science says"**: lead with slides 5, 6, 7, 16; shorten slides 10-12 to one summary slide.
To make a variant: edit the first line of slide 1 in SLIDES in tools/build_talk_deck.py and re-run. Talk track changes live in the notes.

## Claims deliberately left out
- "Your brain is more powerful than any AI" as a stated fact: the deck says efficient and adaptable instead (slide 4 note).
- Goldfish attention-span comparisons (myth) and the "phone on the desk drains capacity" finding (replications mixed).
- Laptop vs handwritten notes (replications mixed).
