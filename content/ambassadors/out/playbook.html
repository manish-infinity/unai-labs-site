unai-labs.com

Share UnAI Labs with your campus. Your friends get 10% off. You earn a share of every sale made with your personal code.

UnAI Labs makes short, science-backed online courses in plain language: AI literacy, deep focus, neuroplasticity and brain health, plus the eBook How to Be a Hippie. Our belief: your brain is more powerful than any AI, if you train it.

The honest part first: this is commission only. There is no salary. If nobody buys with your code, you earn nothing. There is no fee to join and you never have to buy anything.

Need help? Email manish.singh@unai-labs.com with your question (and your code, if you have one). We reply within 2 working days.

Who can join

  • College or university students in India, aged 18 or older, any year, any stream.
  • You need your official college email (for example name@college.ac.in). Personal emails like Gmail are not accepted, because the college email is how we confirm you are a student.
  • Community builders without a college email (club organisers, tutors, group admins) cannot apply online yet. Write to us and we will tell you when that opens.

How it works

  1. Create a free UnAI Labs account (or log in).
  2. Apply on this page with your college name and college email.
  3. Open the confirmation link we send to your college email (valid for 48 hours), while logged in.
  4. Get your code and dashboard. College emails ending in .ac.in, .edu.in or .edu are approved straight away (up to 5 ambassadors per college). Others are reviewed by hand within 3 working days.

When a sale counts

  • Your code must be on the order when it is paid. Your share link adds it automatically. If a buyer removes it or uses a different code, the sale cannot be added later.
  • 7-day wait: a sale is confirmed 7 days after payment, because buyers can ask for a refund in that time. A refunded sale does not count.
  • Your own purchases never count, and your code will not work for you.

How you get paid

  • Add your UPI ID on your dashboard. The name on your UPI account should match your name on UnAI Labs.
  • We pay by UPI once a month, in the first week of the month, when your confirmed earnings are Rs 500 or more. Smaller amounts carry over to the next month.
  • Your dashboard shows every number: sales, your current percentage, pending (inside the 7 days), confirmed, paid so far and balance due.
  • Questions about a payment: write to manish.singh@unai-labs.com with your code.

Tax, in plain words

  • What you earn is your income. Declaring it is your responsibility.
  • We do not deduct tax from your payouts today. If the law ever requires us to, we will tell you before any payout it affects.
  • Ask us any time for a summary of what you were paid in a financial year.

The rules

  • Be honest. Share what the courses actually are. No promises of results, jobs or marks.
  • No spam: no mass messages, no posting the same message again and again, no adding people to groups without asking.
  • No fake reviews, no fake accounts, no paid ads in the UnAI Labs name, no pretending to be UnAI Labs staff.
  • Breaking a rule means your code is switched off. Sales made honestly before that are still paid.

Your data

  • We keep only what the programme needs: your account, college name and college email, city, course and year, and your UPI ID for payouts.
  • Only UnAI Labs can see it. It is never shown to other ambassadors or buyers, and never sold.
  • You never see your buyers' names or details either: your dashboard shows numbers only.
  • Want to leave or have your data deleted? Write to manish.singh@unai-labs.com. Any confirmed balance is paid in the next payout.
