# The Stay Engine: why people keep watching, and how every UnAI video uses it

Owner: Claude (CMO). Applies to every reel, ad, short, podcast clip. Agents use this as the checklist. Version 1, 2026-11-07.

## The brain science (plain words)
1. **Orienting reflex (first 0.5 s).** The brain snaps to anything new, moving, high-contrast or face-like before we decide to look (Sokolov's orienting response). So frame 1 has motion, a face (Neuro, Bit, Chai or Manish), strong colour and a sound hit. Never open on a logo or a still.
2. **Threat first (negativity bias + loss aversion).** A possible loss grabs more attention than an equal gain (Kahneman and Tversky 1979; Baumeister et al. 2001 "Bad is stronger than good"). Hook = what you could lose: your thinking, your job edge, your focus, your sleep. Always resolve the fear with a way out; fear without a fix makes people leave and distrust us.
3. **Curiosity gap.** An open question feels like an itch; the brain keeps watching to close it (Loewenstein 1994; Kang et al. 2009 found curiosity lights up reward areas and boosts memory). Open a loop early ("83% couldn't...") and pay it off later, never in the first second.
4. **Reward prediction (dopamine).** Dopamine spikes on rewards that are a little better or sooner than expected (Schultz 1997). So something new lands every 2 to 3 seconds: a cut, a zoom, a number, a joke, a sound. Predictable = scroll.
5. **"This is about me" (self-relevance).** The brain prioritises anything about the self (self-reference effect; medial prefrontal cortex). Use "you", real Indian situations: the office, the exam hall, the 1 am scroll, the boss's message.
6. **Faces and voices.** We are wired to track eyes and human voices. Characters always have eyes on camera; a voice is on screen within 1 second; Manish's face beats any graphic.
7. **Ears hold you too.** Beat-synced cuts, sound effects on every change, and a voice with attitude. Many people watch muted, so captions carry the story alone; many listen, so audio must carry it alone too.
8. **Easy to process.** One idea per screen, max 7 words big, high contrast. Hard-to-read = "not for me".
9. **Identity and the better future.** People act to become who they want to be. Close on the future self: sharper, harder to replace, calmer. Then one action.
10. **Loops and shares.** Platforms push videos people finish, rewatch, save and send. End so the last frame flows into the first, and give one line worth sending to a friend.

## The rule we never break
Brain-rot works because it is fast, absurd, repetitive and character-driven. We copy the mechanics, not the emptiness: every video carries one true, sourced, useful idea. No fake stats, no fake urgency, no fear without a fix.

## Formats (the bigger plan)
- **A. Neuro vs Bit shorts** (animated, Claude produces end to end): daily. Recurring gags: Bit offers to "think for you", Neuro flexes. Catchphrase: "Switch your brain ON."
- **B. Manish + animation** (Manish records, Claude edits): talking head with punch-in zooms every 2-3 s, Neuro/Bit popping in to react, cards and numbers flying in, captions. 30-45 s.
- **C. "Me vs AI" show / podcast**: Manish against Bit. Bit gives the confident AI answer, Manish tests it with brain science. 10-15 min on YouTube, cut into 3-5 shorts. Later: live "Brain vs ChatGPT" challenges with guests and students.
- **D. Paid ads**: the best-scoring A and B pieces, boosted under the Rs 3,000 cap.

## How Manish records (B and C)
Phone vertical, 1080p or 4K, 30 fps, eye level, face a window, phone or clip mic close, quiet room. Clap once at the start, pause 1 s before and after each line, 3 takes of each hook. For the podcast, also record horizontal. Send files by attaching them in chat, or (better for big files) add a folder such as Movies/unai-raw with the "Add folder" button in the desktop app.
Known gap: the cloud workspace cannot download speech-to-text models (huggingface.co is blocked), so auto-captions need either huggingface.co allowed in Settings > Capabilities, or the work done on the Mac through a connected folder, or Manish reading a script Claude wrote (captions come from the script).

## Audio rules
Our ads use original beats and sound design (safe for paid boosts). Trending sounds: for boosted ads Meta only allows commercially cleared audio (Meta Sound Collection), so we keep our own track. For organic reels, add a trending sound from Instagram's commercial library in the app at low volume under our voice.

## Revenue DNA (Manish, 7 Nov: "every action maps to revenue")
- Every reel, post and email names its product, offer, tracked link and rupee value before it is made. Done = a click, reply or order, not a finished file.
- Formula: HOOK (under 2 s) -> PROOF (named source) -> TURN (the way out) -> OFFER (one product, real price, real deadline or limit) -> ACTION (one tracked link).
- Push hard on: loss aversion, curiosity gap, future self, real scarcity (launch price to 30 Nov, FOUNDING100 100 seats), identity, authority, reciprocity, small first yes.
- Never: fake scarcity, invented numbers, fake reviews, promised results. Meta rejects misleading ads and can ban the ad account.
- Style rotation: each reel uses 2-3 of 20 styles; weekly, keep the top two by 3-second hold and link clicks, drop the bottom two.
