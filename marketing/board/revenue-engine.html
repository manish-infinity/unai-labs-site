The success formula (in every piece, no exceptions)

HookUnder 2 s: fear of loss, curiosity, ambition or "that's me". Voice on in 1 s.
ProofOne real, named source on screen. Science is our unfair edge.
TurnThe way out. Fear without a fix makes people leave.
OfferOne product, real price, real deadline or real limit.
ActionOne tracked link. Every view should lead to a click.

Triggers we push hard

  • Loss aversion: what AI, phones and bad sleep cost you
  • Curiosity gap: open the loop, pay it off late
  • Future self and ambition: harder to replace, sharper, calmer
  • Real scarcity: launch price ends 30 Nov; FOUNDING100 has 100 seats
  • Identity: "operator, not operated"; "the second kind"
  • Authority: named studies (MIT, Microsoft, sleep research)
  • Reciprocity and a small first yes: free 5-day course, free guest lecture

Lines we never cross

  • Fake scarcity or fake countdowns
  • Invented numbers, quotes or studies
  • Fake reviews or testimonials
  • Promised results or income

Why: Meta rejects misleading ads and can ban the ad account, which kills the ₹3,000 boost engine. One exposed fake ends a science brand. The truth hits hard enough when it is framed right.
