## Boost plan (Meta cap ₹3,000 total, Manish 10-30)
- Phase 1 test, 3 days: each ad boosted at ₹100/day (₹900 total). Goal: website visits. Placement: Instagram Reels + Stories. Audience: India, 22-40, interests ChatGPT, artificial intelligence, productivity, personal development, online learning.
- Kill rule after ₹300 per ad: link CTR under 0.8% or cost per link click over ₹15 = stop that ad.
- Phase 2, 7 days: the winner gets the remaining ₹2,100 (₹300/day). Pause if no sale by ₹1,000 spent on it (KB rule: cost per sale over ₹1,000).
- Iterate daily: swap only the hook (first 2.5 s) of the losing ad; next hooks to test: "Your brain on ChatGPT, scanned." / "Stop outsourcing your thinking." / "The skill AI can't replace is yours."
